# Preferences

Standing taste calls a person has made. **Not** decisions (which have a product
reason and live in `decisions.md`) and **not** lessons (which are dated and
episodic).

A preference binds until a person changes it. It needs no justification beyond
someone having said so — which is exactly why a rejection does not belong here.
**When a person rejects a specific piece of work and says why, that is a judged
example and it goes to `lessons/`.** Filing it here instead keeps the verdict and
loses the reason, and the reason is the part the next screen needs.

```
## <the preference>
**Said by:** who, when.
**Scope:** everything / this surface / this component.
```
